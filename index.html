🪶Witek's Birding Hub

The places I check for observations, identification help, and bird news — with a focus on the Netherlands.

Observations & Sightings

  • Waarneming.nl NL Dutch observation database — sightings, maps, species lists
  • Observation.org International sibling of waarneming.nl — global sightings
  • eBird Cornell Lab's global checklist platform — hotspots, alerts, life list
  • Dutch Birding NL Rare bird news and reports from the Netherlands
  • Trektellen.nl NL Migration counts from watchpoints across Europe

Identification

  • Merlin Bird ID Photo, sound, and step-by-step ID by the Cornell Lab
  • All About Birds Comprehensive field guide with sounds and behavior
  • Xeno-canto Massive open archive of bird sounds — search by species or location
  • Macaulay Library Cornell's audio and photo archive — millions of recordings

Conservation & Research

  • Sovon NL Dutch centre for ornithology — counts, atlases, research
  • Vogelbescherming Nederland NL BirdLife partner in the Netherlands — protection & advocacy
  • BirdLife International Global partnership of bird conservation organisations
  • RSPB UK Royal Society for the Protection of Birds — guides & reserves

Reference

  • IOC World Bird List Authoritative global taxonomy and naming
  • Avibase Database of every bird species — checklists, synonyms, taxonomy
